      How to read the map colors

      Ochre: proposed walk [H], using rover stops [R] where available. Dashed blue: rover traverse [R]. Jezero grid: green means sampled slope below the project warning threshold; yellow and red contain steeper samples [D/S]. Gray means local terrain is unknown. No color certifies safety or measures radiation. Gale and Meridiani have no local slope grid in this prototype. No local radiation hazard layer is available.

      NASA map layers are unavailable right now. Route geometry remains visible; inspect terrain before drawing conclusions.

      Labels: R real (NASA/USGS) D derived by us from real data S simulated H hypothetical. Data: Mars Trek (NASA/JPL), USGS Astrogeology, Perseverance and Curiosity (NASA/JPL-Caltech), HiRISE (NASA/JPL/University of Arizona). Background: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS (PIA26680). Sources.

Loading terrain and preparation…

Each destination uses its own real HiRISE elevation grid. Surface texture is orbital imagery [R]. Added rocks, boxwork detail, habitat and support rover are illustrative [H], not surveyed objects. Approach a trail specimen and press E to inspect. Science markers use approximate rover-stop locations. This educational simulation does not predict real survival.

Adaptive exploration pace [S]
Time is compressed by up to 20x for exploration, so walking stays brisk even uphill. Visual movement is limited to 5 m/s on foot and 8 m/s in the support-rover model. The same simulated time drives movement, the clock and resource use. Esc pauses the EVA.
